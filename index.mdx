Flowy is an AI creative-generation platform built around a multi-modal infinite canvas and a **Studio** timeline editor. You generate and connect text, image, video, audio, and interactive content in one place, then collaborate on it in real time.

Move from prompt to pixel, frame to film, idea to outcome in one connected canvas — a multi-modal infinite canvas where your thoughts, prompts, pixels, and motion stay in continuity.
