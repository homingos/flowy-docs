**Studio** is Flowy's timeline editor for composing your assets into a finished, exportable video. When the canvas is where you generate and connect ideas, Studio is where you arrange them into something you can render and share.

You enter **Studio** from the canvas using the mode-switch pill at the top of your workspace. A **Composition** node also opens directly in **Studio**, so anything you assemble on the canvas is ready to edit and play.

## The Studio layout

**Studio** brings together four working areas so you can preview, arrange, fine-tune, and animate in one place.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Video player" icon="film" href="/studio/overview">
    Preview your composition and scrub through it to find the exact frame you need.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Timeline" icon="layer-group" href="/studio/timeline">
    Stack tracks and clips, then trim them, with waveform and frame-strip previews to guide your cuts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Inspector" icon="gear" href="/studio/overview">
    Edit properties across the **Media**, **Nodes**, and **Plugins** tabs to dial in each element.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Animator" icon="bolt" href="/studio/overview">
    Add keyframes and shape motion with easing curves for smooth, intentional animation.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Working on more than one cut? **Scenes** let you keep multiple compositions side by side and switch between them without leaving **Studio**.

<Tip>
  Trim and arrange clips on the **Timeline** first, then use the **Animator** to add polish with keyframes and easing.
</Tip>

When your edit is ready, export turns it into a downloadable video.
