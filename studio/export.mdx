---
title: "Export & render"
description: "Render your composition to a downloadable video."
---

Export renders your **Studio** composition into a single, downloadable video, powered by Remotion. When your timeline looks right, you turn it into a finished file you can share or post anywhere.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open export">
    With your composition open in **Studio**, open the export panel to set up your render.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a resolution">
    Pick the output resolution that fits where the video is headed. Higher resolutions look sharper but take longer to render.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the render">
    Kick off the render. Flowy processes your timeline frame by frame into a video file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download when it's ready">
    Once the render finishes, download your video and it's yours to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Rendering takes time, and longer compositions at higher resolutions take longer still. Renders use credits where applicable. See [Credits](/billing/credits) for how the credit wallet works.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Keep the project open until the render finishes. Closing it before your video is ready can interrupt the render.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Want a cleaner cut before you export? Trim and arrange your clips on the [timeline](/studio/timeline) first.
</Tip>
