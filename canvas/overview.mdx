The **Canvas** is your infinite workspace for creating and connecting nodes. Everything you generate, arrange, and wire together lives here, so your thoughts, prompts, pixels, and motion stay in continuity.

## Key areas

### Toolbar

The toolbar is where you add nodes, switch between select and pan, and zoom in or out. Reach for it whenever you want to drop a new **Text**, **Image**, or **Video** node onto the board.

### Node area

The node area is the infinite surface that fills most of your screen. You can pan and zoom freely, spreading work across as much space as you need. Each node has an output you connect to another node's input, which is how ideas flow from one step to the next.

### AI chat sidebar

The AI chat assistant lives in the sidebar on the right. It proposes a plan, shows confirm-generation cards, and can add or edit nodes straight from your prompt. You can also pick a model backend, so the assistant runs on Claude, OpenAI, or Gemini.

### Mode-switch pill

The mode-switch pill lets you move between **Canvas** and **Studio**. When you build a Composition on the canvas, switch to **Studio** to refine it on a timeline and export a finished video.

<Tip>
The fastest way to add a node is **Quick Creates**. Pick a tab, write a prompt, and your result lands on the canvas in a few clicks.
</Tip>
