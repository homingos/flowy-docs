The image generator turns a text prompt into a finished image, powered by Google Gemini. You describe what you want, set your aspect ratio and resolution, and the result lands on your canvas as an Image node.

You can reach it from **Quick Creates** for a fast single asset, or ask the canvas AI chat to generate one inside a larger flow.

## Options

Pick the aspect ratio that matches where the image is headed.

| Aspect ratio | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| `1:1` | Square |
| `2:3` | Portrait |
| `3:4` | Portrait |
| `9:16` | Portrait |
| `3:2` | Landscape |
| `4:3` | Landscape |
| `16:9` | Landscape |

For resolution, choose `1K`, `2K`, or `4K`. Higher resolutions give you more detail to work with, so reach for `4K` when you need a crisp, large output and `1K` when you just want a quick draft.

## Prompting tips

A clear prompt is the fastest way to a great image.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Be specific">
    Spell out exactly what you want to see. Vague prompts give vague results, so name the subject, the setting, and any important details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe subject, style, and lighting">
    Call out the main subject, the visual style (for example photoreal, watercolor, or 3D render), and the lighting (for example soft morning light or dramatic backlight).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reference image">
    Drop in a reference image to keep characters, products, or color palettes consistent across generations.
  </Step>
</Steps>
