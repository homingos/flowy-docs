The video generator turns a text prompt into a short video clip, powered by Google Veo. You write a prompt, set a few options, and the finished clip lands on your canvas as a Video node.

Open it from **Quick Creates** and pick the **Video** tab, or ask the canvas AI chat to create a video for you.

## Options

| Setting | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Duration | `4s`, `6s`, `8s` |
| Resolution | `720p`, `1080p` |
| Aspect ratio | `16:9`, `9:16`, `1:1` |

Use `16:9` for landscape, `9:16` for vertical social clips, and `1:1` for square formats.

## Prompting tips

A good video prompt describes more than a still image. Spell out what moves and how the shot feels.

- **Subject**: name the main thing in frame and what it is doing.
- **Motion**: describe the action and its speed, like a slow drift or a quick turn.
- **Camera movement**: call out the shot, such as a slow push-in, a pan, or a static lock-off.
- **Mood**: set the lighting, time of day, and overall tone.

<Tip>
Keep one clear action per clip. Short, focused prompts render more reliably than crowded ones.
</Tip>
