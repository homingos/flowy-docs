Flam generates interactive HTML and motion experiences you can drop on your canvas or use in **Studio**. Flam is Flowy's interactive HTML and React media type, so each result is a living, animated piece rather than a flat clip.

You generate Flam from the **Flam** tab in **Quick Creates**, and results land on your canvas as nodes.

## Options

Flam keeps the controls light so you can move fast.

| Option | Values |
| --- | --- |
| Duration | `5s`, `10s` |
| Greenscreen / alpha source | Optional, for clean compositing |

Add a greenscreen or alpha source when you want a transparent background, so your Flam composites cleanly over other assets in **Studio**.

## Generate a Flam

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write a prompt">
    Describe the interactive or motion experience you want to create.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the duration">
    Choose `5s` or `10s` for your experience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Start the generation and let Flowy build your Flam.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and add to your canvas">
    Review the result, then add it to your canvas as a node.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Flam is an interactive, experimental media type. Expect to iterate as you explore what it can do.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Ready to put your Flam to work? Bring it into [Studio](/studio/overview) to compose a finished video, or browse the other generators in the [generators overview](/generators/overview).
</Tip>
