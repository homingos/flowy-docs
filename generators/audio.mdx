---
title: "Audio generator"
description: "Generate speech, music, and sound effects."
---

The audio generator creates speech, music, or sound effects from a prompt. Pick a mode, write what you want to hear, set the options, and your audio lands on the canvas as a node.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Speech">
    Use **Speech** to turn written narration or dialogue into a voice. Type your script, choose a voice, and generate.

    | Voice | Character |
    | --- | --- |
    | Kore | Firm female |
    | Charon | Calm male |
    | Puck | Upbeat male |
    | Zephyr | Bright female |
    | Aoede | Warm female |
    | Fenrir | Strong male |
    | Leda | Youthful female |

    You can also pick a speech model: `gemini-flash`, `gemini-pro`, or `elevenlabs`.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Music">
    Use **Music** to compose a track from a prompt. Choose a sub-type: `Instrumental`, `Song`, or `Pro Song`.

    Shape the mood with tag presets like `Lo-fi`, `Cinematic`, `Trap`, `Ambient`, `Piano`, `Electronic`, `Hip-hop`, and `Orchestral`.

    For songs, structure the lyrics with tags: `Intro`, `Verse`, `Chorus`, `Bridge`, and `Outro`.

    Instrumental durations are `15`, `30`, `60`, `120`, `180`, and `240` seconds.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="SFX">
    Use **SFX** to generate a single sound effect. Describe the sound you want, then set a duration.

    SFX durations are `5`, `10`, `15`, `20`, and `30` seconds.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
