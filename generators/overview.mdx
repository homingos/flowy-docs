**Quick Creates** is the fastest way to generate a single asset in Flowy. You pick a tab, write a prompt, set a few options, and generate. Results land right back on your canvas as nodes, ready to connect and build on.

Open **Quick Creates** from the canvas, choose the asset type you want, and you're off.

## Generators

Each asset type has its own tab with options tuned for that medium. Explore the details for the one you need.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Image" icon="image" href="/generators/image">
    Generate stills in your chosen aspect ratio and size. Powered by Google Gemini.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Video" icon="video" href="/generators/video">
    Generate short clips with a target duration and resolution. Powered by Google Veo.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Audio" icon="music" href="/generators/audio">
    Create speech, music, or sound effects with voices, tags, and durations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Flam" icon="wand-magic-sparkles" href="/generators/flam">
    Generate interactive HTML experiences, with optional greenscreen compositing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Storyboard" icon="film" href="/generators/storyboard">
    Plan a narrative or shot list as a sequence of frames.
  </Card>

## Also available

A few more tabs round out **Quick Creates**:

- **Text** — quick text generation for copy, ideas, and prompts, dropped onto your canvas as a Text node.
- **Compositor** — auto-assembles your selected assets into a [Studio](/studio/overview) timeline, so you can jump straight from generating to editing.

<Warning>
  **3D Models** is coming soon. The tab is visible in **Quick Creates**, but generation isn't available just yet.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Generating consumes credits, and failed generations are refunded automatically. See [how credits work](/billing/credits) for the full lifecycle.
</Note>
