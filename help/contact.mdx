## What to include

A little context helps us help you faster. When you email us, try to share:

- **What you were trying to do** — the step you were on and what you expected to happen.
- **The project or workspace** — the name so we can find it.
- **Any error message** — copy the exact text if you saw one.
- **A screenshot** if possible — a picture is often worth a thousand words.
