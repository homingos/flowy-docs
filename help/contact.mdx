If you're stuck, email the Flowy team at [contact@tryflowy.ai](mailto:contact@tryflowy.ai). We read every message and aim to get you unblocked quickly. You can also swap tips with other creators in the [Flowy Discord](https://discord.gg/nTeVdHpZ).

## Before you reach out

Many issues have a quick fix, so it's worth a look first:

- [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) — fixes for generation, export, and access hiccups.
- [FAQ](/help/faq) — short answers to the most common questions.
- [Credits & billing](/billing/credits) — how the wallet, top-ups, and refunds work.

## What to include

A little context helps us help you faster. When you email, share:

- **What you were trying to do** — the step you were on and what you expected to happen.
- **The project or workspace** — the name, so we can find it.
- **Any error message** — copy the exact text if you saw one.
- **A screenshot** — a picture is often worth a thousand words.

<Note>
  Did a generation fail? You don't need to contact us about that — Flowy refunds failed generations automatically. See [refunds](/help/refunds).
</Note>

Reach us any time at [contact@tryflowy.ai](mailto:contact@tryflowy.ai).
