## Top up your wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Payment">
    Go to the **Payment** area in your dashboard. This is where your credit wallet lives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how much to add">
    Pick an amount that fits your needs. A bigger top-up keeps you going longer between visits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete checkout">
    Pay securely through the checkout flow. Your payment details are handled at the point of purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credits land in your wallet">
    Your balance updates and you can keep generating right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Credits power your generations. Each generation reserves credits when it starts, then deducts them on success or refunds them automatically if it fails. See [how the wallet works](/billing/credits) for the full lifecycle.
</Note>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I buy credits?">
    You buy credits from the **Payment** surface in your dashboard. That is the single place to view your balance and top up whenever you want more.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much do credits cost?">
    Pricing can change, so the most reliable place to check is in the app. See the current pricing at [tryflowy.ai](https://tryflowy.ai) for up-to-date details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My payment didn't go through">
    Try the checkout again first. If it still fails, check that your card details are correct and that your card has not expired. Still stuck? [Contact support](/help/contact) and we will help you sort it out.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Want to know more before you top up? Learn [how the credit wallet works](/billing/credits), or read about [refunds](/help/refunds) if a generation didn't complete.
</Tip>
