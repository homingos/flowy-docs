You sign in to Flowy with Google, GitHub, Figma, or a magic link sent to your email. There is nothing to install, and Flowy runs in your browser. Once you are in, your workspace is ready and you can start a project right away.

## Get into Flowy in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a sign-in method">
    Head to [tryflowy.ai](https://tryflowy.ai) and pick how you want to sign in: **Google**, **GitHub**, **Figma**, or an email **magic link**. Use whichever you already trust, and Flowy handles the rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    If Flowy prompts you to confirm your email, open the message we send and click the link inside. This quick check keeps your account secure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land in your workspace">
    Flowy sets up your workspace automatically, so you arrive ready to create. Your workspace holds your projects and members, and you can start a new project right from there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I didn't get my magic-link email">
    No worries, this is usually quick to fix.

    - Check your spam or junk folder.
    - Wait a moment, since email can take a little time to arrive.
    - Request another magic link from the sign-in screen.

    If it still does not show up, [contact support](/help/contact) and we will help you get in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change how I sign in?">
    Sign in with the same provider you used to sign up. For example, if you joined with **Google**, keep signing in with **Google** so you always land in the same account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I switch workspaces?">
    Use the workspace switcher to move between the workspaces you belong to. To learn more about creating, switching, and inviting members, see [Workspaces](/collaboration/workspaces).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
