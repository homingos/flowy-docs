You can download individual assets straight from the canvas, and you can render a finished video from **Studio**. Both paths are quick, and your downloads land as standard files you can use anywhere.

## Download an asset

Use this when you just want one generated image, clip, or audio file from the canvas.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the node">
    Click the node on the canvas that holds the asset you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download it">
    Use the node's download action to save the file to your device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Export a video

Use this when you want a finished, composed video rather than a single asset. You assemble your clips on the **Studio** timeline, then render the whole composition to a downloadable file (powered by Remotion).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the composition in Studio">
    Switch to **Studio** and open the composition you want to finish. See [the timeline guide](/studio/timeline) for arranging your clips.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Render and download">
    Start the render, then download the finished video. Full details live in the [export guide](/studio/export).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I download an image or clip?">
    Select the node that holds it on the canvas, then use the node's download action. The asset saves as a standard file to your device.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I export a finished video?">
    Open your composition in **Studio** and render it. The render assembles your timeline into a single downloadable video. See [the export guide](/studio/export) for the full walkthrough.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My render is taking a long time">
    Longer and higher-resolution videos take more time to render, so this is usually expected. Keep the project open while it runs so the render can finish. If it stalls or fails, head to [troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) for next steps.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Ready to render? The [Studio export guide](/studio/export) walks you through every option step by step.
</Tip>
