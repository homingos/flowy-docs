## Write better prompts

Most results improve the moment your prompt gets more specific. Tell the generator exactly what you want.

- Describe the **subject** clearly: who or what is in the scene.
- Name a **style**: photo-real, illustration, cinematic, flat, hand-drawn.
- Set the **mood**: lighting, color, energy, time of day.
- Add a **reference image** when you want consistency across generations.
- Iterate. Run it, read the result, and refine one detail at a time.

<Tip>Change one thing per attempt. Small, deliberate edits make it easy to see what each tweak does.</Tip>

## Pick the right generator

Each generator is tuned for a different kind of output, so matching the task to the tool saves time and credits.

- Use **Image** for stills, frames, and concept art.
- Use **Video** for motion and short clips.
- Use **Audio** for speech, music, and sound effects.
- Use **Flam** for interactive, motion-rich HTML pieces.

Browse them all in the [generators overview](/generators/overview), or jump straight to [Image](/generators/image) or [Video](/generators/video).

## When a generation fails

A failed generation is not a lost credit. Flowy refunds it automatically, so you can simply try again.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Simplify your prompt">
    Trim extra detail and keep the core idea. A shorter, clearer prompt often succeeds where a crowded one stalls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry">
    Run it again. Many hiccups are temporary and clear on a second attempt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give video extra time">
    Video takes longer to render than images. Let it finish before assuming something went wrong.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>If failures keep happening, check [troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting). To confirm a credit came back, see how [refunds](/help/refunds) work.</Note>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why did my generation come out different than expected?">
    Generators interpret your words, so vague prompts leave room for surprises. Be specific about subject, style, and mood, and add a reference image to anchor the look. Then iterate one detail at a time until it matches your intent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I make results more consistent?">
    Reuse the same reference image and keep your style and mood wording steady across attempts. Change only the detail you want to adjust, and you will hold the overall look while you refine.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
