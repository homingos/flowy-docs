Here are quick answers to the most common Flowy questions. For more depth, follow the links into the rest of the docs.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Flowy?">
    Flowy is an AI creative platform built around a multi-modal infinite **Canvas** and a **Studio** timeline editor. You generate on the canvas, compose on the timeline, and export a finished result — all in one connected space. New to it? Start with [concepts](/getting-started/concepts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to install anything?">
    No. Flowy runs in your browser — there's no download, no self-hosting, and no offline mode.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What can I generate?">
    Text, images, video, audio, vector graphics, 3D models, storyboards, and interactive **Flam** experiences — each with its own node type on the canvas. See the [nodes overview](/canvas/nodes/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do credits work?">
    Every workspace has a credit wallet, and generating spends from it. Paid plans include a monthly allowance, and you can top up from $5 on any plan. Credits never expire. Full details in [how credits work](/billing/credits).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if a generation fails?">
    Credits are reserved when a generation starts and deducted only on success. If it fails, the reserved credits refund automatically — you're never charged for failed work. See [refunds](/help/refunds).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do the paid plans add?">
    A monthly credit allowance plus higher caps on projects, seats, brand kits, published apps, export quality, and plan mode. First-time subscribers get a 7-day free trial, and annual billing gives you 2 months free. Compare them on [plans & pricing](/billing/plans).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I work with my team?">
    Yes. You collaborate inside workspaces with **Owner**, **Editor**, and **Viewer** roles, plus live presence, multiplayer cursors, comments, and voice rooms. Learn more in [workspaces](/collaboration/workspaces).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get my work out of Flowy?">
    Download individual assets straight from canvas nodes, or render a finished video from Studio. See [exporting & downloads](/help/exporting) and the [export guide](/studio/export).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a mobile app?">
    Flowy works best in modern desktop browsers. The Canvas and Studio aren't optimized for small mobile screens.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I reach support?">
    Email [contact@tryflowy.ai](mailto:contact@tryflowy.ai) — see [contact support](/help/contact) for what to include so we can help fast.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Something not working? Head to [troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) for step-by-step fixes.
</Note>
