Invite people to your workspace or project, give them a role, and you can edit together live. Roles are **Owner**, **Editor**, and **Viewer** only, so access stays simple to reason about.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I invite someone?">
    Invite members to your workspace, and they can open the projects inside it.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open your workspace members">
        Head to your workspace settings, where members live.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Send the invite">
        Add the person by email and send the invitation.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set their role">
        Give them **Editor** if they should create and generate, or **Viewer** if they should only look.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    For the full walkthrough, see [workspaces](/collaboration/workspaces).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't someone edit my project?">
    They likely have the **Viewer** role, which is view-only. Give them the **Editor** role, or approve their access request, and they will be able to create and edit nodes, work on the canvas, and generate. See what each role can do in [roles](/collaboration/roles).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I request access?">
    A **Viewer** can request access to a project, and the **Owner** approves it. Once approved, you can start editing right away. Learn how the request-and-approve flow works in [sharing](/collaboration/sharing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can we work at the same time?">
    Yes. Live presence and multiplayer cursors keep everyone in sync, so simultaneous edits land together and you can see who is working where. You can also leave comments on the canvas to talk things through.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>**Viewers** are view-only until granted access. They can request access, and an **Owner** approves it before they can edit.</Warning>

<Tip>Not sure who should be a **Viewer**, an **Editor**, or the **Owner**? Compare what each one can do in [roles](/collaboration/roles).</Tip>
