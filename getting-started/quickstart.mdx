Quickstart walks you from creating your account to your first AI generation on the canvas. Follow the steps below and you will have an asset on your canvas in just a few minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign in with **Google**, **GitHub**, **Figma**, or a magic link sent to your email. If you used the magic link, verify your email when prompted, and you are in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your workspace">
    Your workspace holds your projects and team members. Create a fresh workspace to start your own, or accept an invite to join a team that already has one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    Inside your workspace, create a new project. A new project opens directly into the **Canvas**, your infinite node-based workspace.

    <Frame caption="A fresh project opens straight into the canvas">
      <img src="/images/new-project-canvas.png" alt="A new Flowy project opening into the canvas" />
    </Frame>
    {/* TODO: capture real screenshot */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate your first asset">
    Open **Quick Creates** and pick a generator like Image or Text. Write a prompt, set your options, and generate. Your result drops onto the canvas as a node, ready to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and build">
    Wire nodes together by connecting one node's output to another's input. When you want to go further, open the **AI chat** in the sidebar to plan, add, and edit nodes from a single prompt.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  New accounts start with free credits so you can explore and generate right away. You can top up anytime from the billing area.
</Note>

<Tip>
  New to the model? Skim the [core concepts](/getting-started/concepts) to learn the building blocks, then browse the [generators overview](/generators/overview) to see everything you can create. 🚀
</Tip>
