Flowy projects use exactly three roles: **Owner**, **Editor**, and **Viewer**. Each role sets what a person can do inside a project, from full control to view-only.

## What each role can do

Here is how the three roles compare.

| Role | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full control, including deleting the project and managing access. |
| **Editor** | Create and edit nodes and the canvas, and generate. |
| **Viewer** | View-only, and can request access. |

</Warning>

## How access is granted

A Viewer never gets edit access on their own. An **Owner** always makes the call.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How access is granted">
    An **Owner** grants access in one of two ways. The Owner can invite people directly and pick their role, or the Owner can approve an access request that a **Viewer** has sent. Either way, the new permission takes effect as soon as the Owner confirms it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
