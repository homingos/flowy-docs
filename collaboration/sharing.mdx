Share a project and your whole team can work on it together, in real time, on the same canvas. Everyone sees the same nodes, cursors, and edits as they happen.

## Share a project

Open the project you want to share, then invite people by email and pick a role for each person.

- **Owner** has full control, including delete and managing access.
- **Editor** can create and edit nodes, work the canvas, and generate.
- **Viewer** gets view-only access and can request edit access.

Invited members get an email and find the project in their workspace once they accept.

## Request and approve access

A **Viewer** who needs to edit can request access right from the project. The request goes to the **Owner**, who approves it and bumps the person up to **Editor**. No need to re-share from scratch.

## Real-time editing

Collaboration on the canvas is fully live. You see each teammate's presence, their multiplayer cursor moving in real time, and every simultaneous edit stays in sync across everyone's screens. There is nothing to refresh and no save button to fight over.

## Comments

Leave feedback right where it belongs. Drop comments directly on the canvas so your notes sit next to the nodes they're about, and your team can reply in context.

## Approve an access request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the request">
    When a **Viewer** requests edit access, you'll get a notification on the project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review who's asking">
    Check the requester so you're granting access to the right person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    Approve the request to promote them to **Editor**, or leave them as a **Viewer** to keep things view-only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Not sure which role to hand out? See the full breakdown in [roles](/collaboration/roles).
</Tip>
