Flowy runs on a credit wallet, and generating spends credits. Every account has its own wallet, so you always know what powers your creations.

## Your wallet

Every account comes with a credit wallet built in. When you sign up, you start with free credits so you can explore the **Canvas**, try the generators, and see what Flowy can do — no setup required.

Your balance lives in the **Payment** area of your dashboard, where you can watch it move as you create.

## What uses credits

Generating content is what spends credits. That includes images, video, audio, and other generations you run from **Quick Creates** or the canvas.

The AI chat assistant works a little differently — it is metered by tokens as it plans, edits nodes, and analyzes your images.

<Note>
  Video is the most credit-intensive generator, so longer, higher-resolution clips spend more than a quick image.
</Note>

## The credit lifecycle

Credits move through three clear states whenever you generate. This keeps your balance honest, even when something goes wrong.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reserved">
    The moment a generation starts, the needed credits are blocked in your wallet so they cannot be spent twice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deducted">
    When the generation succeeds, the reserved credits are charged and the work lands on your canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refunded">
    If the generation fails, the reserved credits are returned to your wallet automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  You are never charged for failed generations. If something fails, the credits are refunded automatically — no support ticket needed.
</Warning>

## Top up

When you want more credits, you top up from the billing area. Head to the **Payment** surface in your dashboard to add to your wallet and keep creating.

<Note>
  For current prices, see the in-app pricing at [tryflowy.ai](https://tryflowy.ai).
</Note>
